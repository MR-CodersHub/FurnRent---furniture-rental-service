Help & FAQs

Rental Questions Answered

Have questions about renting individual pieces, tenure, deposits, or maintenance? We've got you covered.

What is the minimum rental tenure for collection items?
You can rent any furniture piece starting from just 1 month. We offer flexible options of 1, 3, 6, or 12+ months. Choosing longer durations unlocks deeper monthly discounts up to 35% off.
How does the security deposit work?
We charge a small refundable security deposit equal to just 1 month's rental. The entire deposit is credited directly back to your original bank account within 24-48 hours of item pickup.
What if an item gets accidental stains or scratches?
We know life happens! Every order comes with our complimentary FurnCare Damage Waiver covering up to ₹10,000 in normal wear, surface scratches, or accidental stains without penalties.
Can I swap or return an item if it doesn't fit my room?
Yes! We offer a 7-day hassle-free exchange guarantee upon delivery. If the piece doesn't complement your interior or room dimensions, our team will pick it up and swap it for another product.
Can I buy the rented furniture later (Rent-to-Own)?
Absolutely! At any point during your rental tenure, you can choose to purchase the furniture. Up to 70% of the rent you have already paid will be adjusted against the buyout price.
