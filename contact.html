Help & FAQs

Common Questions

Clear answers about deliveries, refundable deposits, 7-step hygiene protocol, and our flexible style swaps.

Delivery

How quickly can you deliver furniture?

We deliver within 48 hours across all 8 service cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad). In Bengaluru, Mumbai and Delhi, we offer same-day express delivery for orders confirmed before 12 PM. Professional white-glove setup and room assembly are always included free.
Guaranteed Speed: Same-day to 48-hour delivery with 100% free technician setup.
Payments

Do I need to pay anything upfront?

You only pay the first month's rent + a refundable security deposit (typically 1 to 2 months' rent depending on your selected tier). That's all. There are zero registration fees, zero delivery charges, and no hidden maintenance costs. Your security deposit is transferred back to your original payment method within 3 working days after pickup.
Transparent Policy: No hidden costs; 100% refundable deposit returned in 3 business days.
Quality

Is the furniture new or refurbished?

All our furniture is either brand new or professionally re-certified to pristine, like-new showroom standard. Every piece undergoes our strict 25-point quality inspection and 7-step hygiene sanitization — including hospital-grade anti-microbial disinfection, deep thermal extraction, and sealed sterile packaging.
Pristine Condition: 25-point quality check & sterile tamper-proof delivery packaging.
Showrooms

Can I see the furniture before renting?

Yes! You can visit our modern flagship showrooms and experience centers in Bengaluru (Koramangala), Mumbai (BKC), and Delhi NCR (Gurugram). Prefer shopping online? Our interactive 3D multi-angle view and true-to-scale dimensions allow you to inspect every stitch, cushion depth, and finish with complete precision.
Experience Centers: Visit in person or browse high-resolution 3D models online.
Relocation

What if I need to move to another apartment or city?

Moving is completely stress-free! If you relocate within your current city, our team will dismantle, pack, transport, and reassemble all your rented furniture at your new address completely free of cost (1 free move per 12-month tenure). Moving intercity? Simply notify us 15 days ahead and we will coordinate doorstep transfer.
Relocation Perk: 1 free intracity relocation including disassembly & setup.
Protection

What if furniture gets accidentally damaged or stained?

We know accidents happen! Every active rental agreement automatically includes our complimentary ₹10,000 Damage Waiver with zero deductible. Minor coffee spills, small fabric scuffs, or everyday wear and tear are 100% covered without reducing your security deposit.
Damage Waiver: ₹10,000 complimentary protection coverage with zero deductibles.
Style Swap

Can I swap my furniture if I want a different look?

Yes! Your home decor should adapt as your taste evolves. After every 12 months of rental tenure, you can request 1 free annual style swap to replace any piece with a brand-new model of equal value. No swap penalties, no extra logistics fees.
Free Annual Upgrade: Swap your furniture every 12 months with zero penalties.
