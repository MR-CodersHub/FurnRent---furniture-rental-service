Our Story

From a Small Idea to India's Fastest-Growing Furniture Rental Platform

FurnRent was born in 2019 when our founder, after relocating to Bengaluru for work, spent ₹80,000 buying furniture for a flat he'd leave in 18 months. The frustration of that experience sparked an idea: why not rent premium furniture instead?

Today, FurnRent serves 12,000+ customers across 25+ Indian cities, offering 500+ premium furniture pieces on flexible monthly rental plans. We've saved our customers an estimated ₹50 crore in unnecessary furniture purchases.

Mission & Values

Our Mission

To make premium, quality furniture accessible to every Indian — whether in a hostel room or a luxury apartment — through flexible, affordable rentals.

A future where no one buys furniture for a rented home. Where living well is a choice, not a financial burden. Circular economy in action.
