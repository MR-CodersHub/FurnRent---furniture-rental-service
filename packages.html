FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the security deposit and is it refundable?
The security deposit is 2 months' rent and is 100% refundable when you return the furniture in its original condition. It's returned within 7 business days of pickup.
Can I add or remove furniture from my plan?
Yes! You can add furniture to your plan at any time. Removing furniture requires 15-day notice and may adjust your monthly rental amount accordingly.
What happens if furniture gets damaged during my rental?
Normal wear and tear is covered at no extra cost. For accidental damage, we assess the situation and either repair or replace the item. A nominal damage charge may apply for severe damage.
How does the furniture swap feature work?
Eligible plan holders can request a swap through their dashboard. We'll pick up the old furniture and deliver the new piece — usually within 48-72 hours. The swap must be for an item of equal or lesser value.
What areas do you deliver to?
We currently serve Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Ahmedabad, Kolkata and 17 more cities. Enter your pincode on the product page to check availability.
Can I rent for just 1 month?
Yes! The Student Starter plan allows a 1-month minimum. Other plans require 3-6 months minimum. 1-month rates are available at a slight premium.
